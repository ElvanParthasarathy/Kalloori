LABORATORY DETAILS
The department has spacious computer centre equipped with 200 Intel i7 systems LAN connected and required software as per Anna University Syllabus to cater to the academic needs of students. Round the clock, 160 Mbps Internet Connectivity by reliance and 60 Mbps Tata telecom internet connectivity is available for the department.

The department has dedicated server for File, Backup, DNS, and Oracle. The lab is equipped with 170 KVA UPS set as a standby. A Cyberoam NGFW-CR15001NG-XP Firewall provides security for the system. To facilitate the hostel students , Wi-Fi connectivity is provided in the hostel premise.
