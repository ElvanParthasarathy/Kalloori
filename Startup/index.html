Introduction
RMDEC Faculty, Staff and Students Startup Policy 2020 is developed based on The National Innovation and Startup Policy 2019 for students and faculty of Higher learning Educational Institutions (HEI). This policy promotes active engagement of students, faculties and staff in innovation and entrepreneurship related activities.
The policy address the
a) Development of Entrepreneurship Cells for Innovation and Startup in RMDEC
b) Norms for the Faculty, Staffs and Students Startups
c) Intellectual Property Rights Protection and licensing and
d) Monitoring of the policy implementation.

1 Summary The RMDEC Faculty, Staffs and Students Startup Policy will enable active engagement of students, faculties and staff in innovation and entrepreneurship related activities. This also Policy outlines the Intellectual Property ownership, thus enabling creation of a robust innovation and Startup ecosystem in the College.
2 Applies to Faculty, Staff and Students of RMDEC
