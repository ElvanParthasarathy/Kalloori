R.M.D. ALUMNI MEET 2025

(For the students graduated from 2005 to 2022)

34th ALUMNI MEET OF R.M.D. ENGINEERING COLLEGE

at Kay-EM Spectra Hotel at Vanagaram, Chennai.

on 16th November 2025, Sunday at 9.00 a.m.


Registration open till 12th November 2025


Guidelines for Registration :


  1. Upload your passport size photo (Less than 100 KB)
  2. Enter your credentials
  3. Take a print out of the generated Alumni Pass.

    4. Important: Only the Alumni members with Alumni Meet Pass Printout (A4 Size) will be allowed to participate.

  4. Registration will be closed on Wednesday, the 12th November 2025 at 5.00 p.m.

Instructions to Participants:


  1. Should be a member of RMDEC Alumni Association (Year of Graduation from 2005 to 2022).
  2. Online registration is compulsory to attend this Alumni meet.
  3. Spot registration is strictly prohibited.
  4. Accompanying family members, friends and relatives are strictly not allowed.
  5. Alumni meet is only for 2005 to 2022 passed out batches.
  6. RMKEC and RMKCET Alumni members are not eligible to participate in this Alumni meet..
  7. Hard copy of the Alumni Pass is mandatory to attend this Alumni meet and must be produced at the registration counter.
  8. Alumni Enrollment Card will be issued during registration time at the venue.
  9. The 2019-2023 and 2020-2024 batches are not permitted to participate in this Alumni Meet.
