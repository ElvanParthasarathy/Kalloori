Principal


Dr.N.Anbuchezhian, an accomplished academician, possesses a rich experience of more than 39 years in Teaching, Research and Administration in the affiliated colleges of Anna University, Chennai. He holds UG Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Masters Degree in Mechanical Engineering (Engineering Design) and Masters Degree in Business Administration. He obtained Ph.D Degree under the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering from College of Engineering, Guindy, Anna University, Chennai.

In addition to teaching and research, he has served in various administrative roles for more than two decades. He was the Convener of Examination Cell in Kongu Engineering College. He served as the Head of the Department (Mechanical Engineering), Academic Coordinator, and Vice-Principal in R.M.K. Engineering College. He also served as Vice-Principal in R.M.D. Engineering College. He assumed charge as Principal of R.M.D. Engineering College since November 2017.

He was the Chairman of Valuation Board (Zone I) in Anna University, Chennai consecutively for four terms. He had been a visiting faculty of Anna University, Chennai. He was a member of Board of Studies of Anna University, Chennai for Curriculum and Syllabi Revision of Regulation 2008. He was a member of Executive Committee for the Controller of Examinations of Anna University of Technology, Coimbatore. He acted as the question paper setter for Anna University end semester Examinations.

He has received funds worth Rs.19 lakhs from the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi for two projects under MODROB scheme. His research area includes Renewable Energy, Customer Voice in Product Design & Development, Materials & Operations Research. He has led more than 30 UG and PG research projects. He has co-convened three International and four National Conferences. He has published 22 Research papers in WoS & Scopus Journals and his Scopus h-index is 8. He is also a recognized Research Supervisor under Anna University.

He has received “Dr.Abdul Kalam Eminent Leadership Award” from The Institution of Engineers, India. He is a lifetime member of the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE), Member of the Institution of Engineers (India), Member of the All India Management Association (AIMA) and Member of the Society of Automotive Engineers. He also holds a Chartered Engineer Certification from the Institution of Engineers, India.

He has a passion to produce intellectuals with strong fundamental knowledge and creative thinking–the need of the hour today. He sets priorities in the context of stakeholder needs. He thrives to brand the ecosystem towards student-centric learning. His aspiration is to prepare the next generation Engineers with various skill sets according to the needs of the Industry.
